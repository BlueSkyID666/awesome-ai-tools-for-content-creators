# Features

What this repository does today, what is committed, and what is only an idea. The three
headings are not the same promise, and the difference is the point of the file.

## Available

Shipping now, in this release.

- **53 tools across 9 stages**, following one piece of work from a blank week to a finished upload.
- **Judged on the deadline trade**, not on best possible output. Twenty minutes to acceptable beats four hours to excellent when the four hours do not exist.
- **Consent, licensing, and disclosure stated plainly**, because voice cloning, generated music, and synthetic media each carry an obligation the schedule does not excuse.
- **Two complete editions.** English and Arabic, same entries, same order, descriptions written in each language rather than translated.
- **Every link resolved over HTTP** before release, and pricing and platform labels from the portfolio's shared vocabularies.

## Planned

Committed. These have a decided shape and will land; nothing is listed here to make the
repository look busier than it is.

- **A pass every quarter**, with the free tiers and the output licences re-read, because both change more often than the products do.
- **Entries for tools the issue tracker asks about**, which is better evidence of a gap than guessing at one.

## Ideas

Not committed, and possibly never. Listed because a contributor may want to argue for one, or
build it. Opening an issue about any of them is welcome.

- **A column for what each generator's licence says about commercial use.** The most consequential difference between these products and the least visible one.
- **A note on which free tiers watermark or cap resolution.** Useful, and it changes with every pricing revision.
- **A section on the business side of creating.** Sponsorship, rates, and contracts are a real need and are advice rather than tooling.

Anything absent from all three lists is absent on purpose. See
